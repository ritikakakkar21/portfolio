Multi-Client Agency: 500%+ Organic Growth with CRO

Running concurrent B2B and D2C accounts on a sprint model that tied every ranking to a conversion.

01

Traffic without revenue

Across a portfolio of B2B and D2C accounts, the pattern repeated: low organic visibility, rankings stuck on pages three and four, and weak conversion even where traffic existed. Marketing spend was inefficient and ROI was hard to defend.

Managing several accounts at once meant the answer had to be a repeatable operating model, not bespoke heroics per client.

02

The sprint model

  1. Sprint-based SEO execution

    Each sprint opened with high-intent keyword targeting, competitor gap analysis and a shortlist of quick wins prioritised by revenue potential.

  2. Content and on-page rebuild

    Rebuilt the money pages around keyword targeting, clear content structure and strong calls to action.

  3. CRO and landing page optimization

    Improved layout, CTA placement and user journey, then validated changes with A/B testing rather than opinion.

  4. Cross-channel alignment

    Aligned SEO with paid and landing-page strategy so messaging stayed consistent across the funnel and every channel reinforced the others.

03

Running it across accounts

As programme manager I led cross-functional teams across SEO, content, design and development, and built the KPI reporting frameworks in GA4, SEMrush and Search Console that kept concurrent accounts comparable and honest.

  • Shared reporting cadence, so every client saw the same metrics in the same shape each month.
  • Prioritisation by revenue impact, not by ticket age.
  • Quick wins shipped inside the sprint while structural work ran in parallel.
