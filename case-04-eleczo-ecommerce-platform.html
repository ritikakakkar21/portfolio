Case 04  /  Eleczo

E-commerce Platform: 238K Clicks and 115% Growth

A large electrical-goods catalog where the money was sitting in category pages nobody had optimized.

01

A catalog competing with itself

Eleczo sells electrical products across a deep catalog. The traffic problem was structural rather than volumetric: category pages carried almost no optimized content, so individual SKUs were left to fight for head terms they could never win, and the pages that aggregate real buying intent had nothing to rank with.

Engagement told the same story — visitors landed, found no orientation and left before reaching a product they could buy.

02

Finding where the demand actually was

I ran keyword research and content-gap analysis in SEMrush and Ahrefs across the whole category tree, then prioritised by commercial value rather than volume.

  1. Category page rebuild

    Wrote and structured content for category pages so they could rank for aggregate intent instead of leaving it to product listings.

  2. On-page systemisation

    Reworked meta titles, descriptions and header hierarchies across the catalog, with internal linking to push authority to priority categories.

  3. Off-page authority

    Ran a link-building programme to strengthen domain authority in a competitive commodity category.

  4. Conversion layer

    Added banners with clear CTAs on landing pages and built a WhatsApp CRM engagement funnel for lead nurturing and repeat purchase.

03

Running the programme to monthly targets

Every month had explicit click, user and new-user targets, reviewed against actuals in GSC and GA. The programme finished the period consistently ahead of plan — between 106% and 126% of target across tracked metrics.

04

What changed on the SERP

Optimized categories started holding top-three positions for high-intent commercial queries, including branded and modular switch terms, while the site's average position across the full keyword set settled at 12.

The commercial impact went beyond sessions: better-qualified traffic plus the WhatsApp nurture funnel lifted the B2B sales pipeline by 40%.

The takeaway

In e-commerce, category pages are the real money pages. They aggregate intent that individual SKUs can never capture on their own.
