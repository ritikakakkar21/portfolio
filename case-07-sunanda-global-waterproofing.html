Waterproofing: Page 1 in One Month

01

A service site with no internal gravity

Sunanda Global sells waterproofing solutions across multiple Indian cities. The content existed — blogs, service pages, product information — but it was disconnected. Nothing pointed at the pages that were supposed to convert.

With a short runway and no appetite for a rebuild, the highest-leverage asset was the site's own link graph.

02

The one-month internal linking sprint

  1. Connect the blog network

    Linked related blog posts to each other to build topical clusters and keep readers moving through the site.

  2. Point content at conversion pages

    Integrated service page CTAs and contextual links inside blog content, so informational traffic had a route to a quote.

  3. Cross-link the service tree

    Added waterproofing links from other service pages to strengthen crawl paths and consolidate relevance signals for the priority category.

  4. On-page and keyword alignment

    Optimized titles, headings and body copy against city-level commercial queries, with keyword research guiding which city pages to push first.

Alongside the technical work I coordinated with the client and their content writer to publish industry-relevant posts, giving the new internal-link structure something worth linking to.
