How I work

Diagnose, systemise, automate, compound

The same four moves behind every case study on this site, whether the client is a six-market e-commerce brand or a one-city service business.

01

Diagnose

Crawl, Core Web Vitals, indexation and intent gaps before a single word gets rewritten. No prescriptions without a diagnosis.

02

Systemise

Templates, keyword clusters and internal-link architecture, so growth comes from a repeatable system instead of heroic one-offs.

03

Automate

LLM and n8n workflows for clustering, drafting and metadata; Apps Script bots for reporting. Machines do the repetition.

04

Compound

Measure classic and AI search surfaces weekly, then reinvest in whatever moves qualified traffic and revenue.

About

Hi, I'm Ritika.

I lead SEO and growth for Sportstech, a fitness equipment brand selling across Germany, France and the wider EU, where I own AI search visibility, programmatic content systems and international migrations.

Before that I managed growth and SEO programmes for a portfolio of B2B and D2C clients at Brandshark, ran e-commerce SEO for a large electricals catalog, and rebuilt search performance for travel and local service businesses.

The thread through all of it: I like the problems where the obvious playbook is blocked — a content freeze, a risky migration, a site with no authority — and the answer has to be a system rather than more output. Lately that means Generative Engine Optimization: structuring content so AI search surfaces can find, quote and attribute it.
