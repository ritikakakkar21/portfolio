90-Day GTM Recovery + GA4-to-Slack Bot

01

A traffic drop with three suspects

Hey Savi is a UK AI-powered fashion search platform. Organic visibility had dropped and the founding team was being offered the usual explanations — algorithm, competition, bad luck. What they needed first was a diagnosis they could trust.

I ran a full technical and content audit across Google Search Console, GA4 and the WordPress build. The decline was not one problem; it was three, stacked.

  • Core Web Vitals regression: Largest Contentful Paint had degraded to 8.7 seconds — far outside the passing threshold, on the pages that mattered most.
  • Google spam update impact: the algorithmic update had hit content and link patterns that needed a quality-first response, not a quick fix.
  • Indexation hygiene: non-production URLs were publicly indexed, splitting signals and exposing unfinished environments to crawlers.
02

The 90-day recovery roadmap

I built a phased 90-day plan with named owners and weekly milestones — deliberately structured so a small team could execute it alongside product work, and so progress was visible every week rather than at the end.

DAYS 1–30

Stabilise

Fix the LCP regression, clean up indexation of non-production URLs, correct category SEO titles and metadata, and establish a measurement baseline.

DAYS 31–60

Rebuild trust signals

Deploy FAQ schema via WPCode, run bulk alt-text and WebP optimization, publish LLMs.txt, apply entity-based on-page optimization and ship the WordPress Press Centre.

DAYS 61–90

Compound

Launch programmatic landing pages for AI visual-search intent, clear the sitelink blockers on the app subdomain, and lock in an automated reporting cadence.

03

Killing the reporting tax

Early-stage teams lose hours every week rebuilding the same numbers. I built a GA4-to-Slack reporting bot using Google Apps Script and Slack Incoming Webhooks that posts the daily performance digest straight into the team channel.

Outcome

100% of recurring performance reporting is now automated. The founders open Slack to yesterday's numbers — no dashboards to log into, no analyst time spent on assembly, and a shared source of truth for daily decisions.

04

Everything else that shipped

Recovery work only counts if it lands in the product. Alongside the roadmap I built and shipped the fixes myself.

  • FAQ schema implemented through WPCode to win richer SERP real estate.
  • Bulk image optimization — alt text at scale plus WebP conversion to claw back load time.
  • LLMs.txt published to guide AI crawler access and improve AI-search discoverability.
  • Entity-based on-page optimization across metadata and body content for AI-driven and voice search.
  • Sitelink recovery: diagnosed and cleared the blockers preventing sitelinks for the app subdomain.
  • WordPress Press Centre designed and built in Avada/Fusion Builder, publishing 14 press articles for brand authority.
  • Programmatic landing pages with custom HTML/CSS targeting AI visual-search intent.
  • Client-ready deliverables: audit reports, a 12-slide GTM strategy deck and brand-voice copy the team could ship without rewrites.

Results

A recovery programme measured by shipped fixes and reclaimed team time.
