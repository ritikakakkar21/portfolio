Case 03  /  Andaman Love

Travel Site: From AI Filler to 460% Click Growth

A travel site with plenty of published content and almost no visibility. The fix started by deleting the shortcut that caused it.

01

Plenty of content, no traction

Andaman Love had a full site of travel content and flat search performance: roughly 25 clicks a day, an average position in the high twenties, and a click-through rate under 1%.

The root cause was easy to spot and uncomfortable to name: the site relied heavily on AI-generated content with no editorial layer. It read like everyone else's, demonstrated no first-hand experience, and gave Google no reason to rank it for competitive travel queries.

Diagnosis

Stagnant traffic and rankings driven by over-reliance on AI-generated copy, weak on-page structure and unresolved technical debt.

02

Rewriting for a human reader

I replaced the auto-generated pages with human-written, SEO-optimized copy in the brand's voice — specific, useful and grounded in real destination detail.

  • Content aligned to E-E-A-T: real experience, specificity and trust signals instead of generic summaries.
  • Readability rebuilt for travel intent — scannable structure, clear itineraries, practical answers.
  • Conversion-focused CTAs placed where planning decisions actually get made.
03

Keyword research and intent mapping

Next, demand mapping. I built the target set from transactional and informational queries using SEMrush and Google Autosuggest, prioritising long-tail terms with genuine conversion potential.

Those queries went into a content matrix that mapped each keyword group to a specific high-intent page, so no two pages competed and every important query had an owner.

04

Structure, speed and schema

  1. Internal linking and topic clusters

    Grouped content into clusters with pillar pages, then linked older blog posts into service pages — reducing crawl depth and lifting engagement.

  2. Technical clean-up

    Resolved crawl errors, fixed sitemap and indexing issues, and cut page load time by around 1.8 seconds.

  3. Structured data

    Added FAQ and LocalBusiness schema to improve SERP presentation and local relevance.

  4. On-page enhancement

    Rewrote titles, meta descriptions and H1–H3 hierarchies, added descriptive alt text, and validated coverage with SurferSEO content scoring.

Results

The takeaway

AI can draft, but it cannot earn trust. Rewriting the site for a human reader turned out to be the highest-ROI technical fix available.
